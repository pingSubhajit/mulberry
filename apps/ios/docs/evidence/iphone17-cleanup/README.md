The phone still uses the existing LAN mock at http://192.168.0.105:8788. No APNs entitlement or remote push was used. USB and Device Hub screen sharing were connected; no debugger was attached.

Revision 5 proved the new completion action removes its own delivered update notification. Two matching Shortcuts ran; one reported a missing-image error. The image appeared on the Home Screen without tapping the notification or manually running the Shortcut. The cleanup log reports one removed update and zero remaining at readback. The app also showed zero delivered update notices.

Disabling notifications in Settings produced Notifications are off when the app resumed. Restoring and changing delivery settings produced Settings changed — test again. These are direct readings of UNNotificationSettings. Screenshots capture both the disabled status and quiet delivery settings.

A missing Shortcut also returned to Mulberry through the error callback and displayed a failure message.

All three disposable lab copies were removed through Shortcuts, leaving the user's Dev Mode and Charger shortcuts unchanged. A single fresh template was imported without the stuck question or duplicate prompt. iOS prefilled Wallpaper 5, so the app now directs the user to check the target explicitly inside Set Wallpaper. First-run prompts for Mulberry actions and wallpaper access both appeared and were allowed. The manual run returned to Mulberry through x-callback-url. Its revision 9 receipt did not mark automatic updates as verified; the app still requested an automatic test.

Revision 10 then completed through the enabled notification trigger, removed its notification, and visibly appeared on the locked screen. The event log includes notification_presented during the lock transition, so this is NOT proof that a quiet notification arrived while the phone was already locked. Quiet delivery on an already-locked phone remains a separate test. The earlier ordinary-notification locked test is recorded in ../iphone17, but must not be used as evidence for this new quiet-delivery condition.

The final phone state has one fresh Mulberry template with Automation enabled and Notify disabled, targeting Wallpaper 5. Notification Center-only delivery remains enabled. The phone was left locked with revision 10 visible. User account and Techaboard team settings were untouched.

The app presents observations, not a claim of continuous health. Notification access is checked while active. A test waits up to 45 seconds for the same revision's final receipt; this is a diagnostic timeout, not an iOS rate limit. A manual run launched from the app invalidates the inference that a later receipt proves the automatic trigger. It cannot distinguish a renamed, deleted, disabled, malformed, or permission-blocked Shortcut from a missing receipt. It cannot read wallpaper pixels or detect a user removing/reordering Set Wallpaper while leaving the receipt action. A user manually running the Shortcut outside the app during a probe is also not distinguishable from its automatic run.

Cleanup selects only delivered notices with kind=wallpaper_update and a positive revision at or below the applied image. Tests verify newer, unrelated, and malformed notices remain. Removal happens only in the final App Intent, after Set Wallpaper in the supplied template, and before best-effort remote telemetry. It does not clear pending notifications or other apps' notifications. It is not a guarantee that a notice never briefly exists in Notification Center.

Fresh Shortcut import and its action permissions were tested; this was not a freshly erased phone or a reset app notification permission. Remote APNs, Focus, Scheduled Summary behavior, Low Power Mode, reboot, overnight use, and sustained/burst rates remain unverified.
