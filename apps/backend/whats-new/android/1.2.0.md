## Put your favourite photos on the canvas, then doodle right over them

Tap the new camera in the toolbar to snap a photo or pick one from your gallery. Make it a polaroid, give it a simple frame, or cut it out like a sticker, then size and turn it just right. Your doodles always land on top, so you can draw a heart around your favourite face. Your partner sees it on their lock screen the next time they pick up their phone.

- **New:** Sign in with Apple, and link Google and Apple to the same account from your profile.
- **Better:** A fresh look for the canvas, with undo and redo up top and all your tools along the bottom.
- **Better:** Press and hold anywhere on the canvas to send a reaction, whatever tool you're using. Slide to the one you want and let go.
- **Better:** The eraser now wipes away every stroke it touches, and the eyedropper shows a magnifier as you pick a colour.
- **Better:** Your streak screen comes alive, and your very first drawing gets a little celebration.
