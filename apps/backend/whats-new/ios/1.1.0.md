## Put your favourite photos on the canvas, then doodle right over them

Tap the new camera in the toolbar to snap a photo or pick one from your library. Make it a polaroid, give it a simple frame, or cut it out like a sticker, then size and turn it just right. Your doodles always land on top, so you can draw a heart around your favourite face. Your partner sees it on their lock screen the next time they pick up their phone.

- **Better:** Tidier canvas controls, and the brush width now shows a scribble as thick as your brush.
- **Fixed:** The wallpaper gallery keeps loading more as you scroll.
